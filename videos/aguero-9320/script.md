## Fact check
- 13 May 2012, Etihad: Man City 3–2 QPR. Zabaleta 39', Cissé 48', Barton sent off 55', Mackie 66', Džeko 90+2 (header), Agüero 93:20 (assist Balotelli, who poked it on as he went down).
  [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/man-city-vs-qpr/238249), [FourFourTwo](https://www.fourfourtwo.com/news/how-it-unfolded-manchester-citys-remarkable-last-gasp-win-over-qpr-in-2012-1652346012000), [Planet Football](https://www.planetfootball.com/quick-reads/man-city-qpr-2012-premier-league-title-unbelievable-stats-records-balotelli-aguero)
- United won 1–0 at Sunderland the same afternoon. City and United finished level on points (89) and City won the title on goal difference, the only time the Premier League title has been decided on goal difference. [Hürriyet/AFP](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/manchester-city-win-english-premier-league-title-20677)
- City's first league title since 1968 (44 years). [ESPN: Agüero statue](https://global.espn.com/football/story/_/id/37628402/man-city-unveil-sergio-aguero-statue-celebrate-iconic-9320-premier-league-title-2011-12)
- QPR were fighting relegation that day (they stayed up as other results went their way).
- Not used: commentary quotes (copyrighted), exact goal-difference figures.
