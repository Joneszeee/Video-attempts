Manchester City. Losing, with ninety minutes gone. And two hundred seconds to save the title.
<break time="0.8s" />
The thirteenth of May, 2012. The final day. City and United were level on points.
<break time="0.8s" />
City just had to beat Queens Park Rangers, at home. QPR were fighting relegation.
<break time="0.8s" />
Zabaleta scored. QPR equalised. Then Joey Barton was sent off.
<break time="0.8s" />
And then, ten-man QPR went two–one up.
<break time="0.8s" />
At Sunderland, United won. The title was going to Old Trafford.
<break time="0.8s" />
In the ninety-second minute, Džeko headed in the equaliser.
<break time="0.8s" />
Then, with almost the last kick of the season, Balotelli poked the ball to Agüero.
<break time="0.8s" />
Ninety-three minutes, twenty seconds. Agüero scored.
<break time="0.8s" />
City's first league title in forty-four years, won on goal difference.
