# Research: what if Bruno Fernandes had left Man United in summer 2024?

## Method (agreed with the user)
1. Premier League only, 2024/25 and 2025/26, real scores for all 760 matches (openfootball), cross-checked against ESPN match data: all 76 United scores match.
2. Every United goal Bruno scored or assisted is deleted (assister taken from ESPN's match commentary, 'Assisted by …').
3. Penalties: a stand-in takes the 7 penalties Bruno scored, at roughly the league average (~75%): in date order, every 4th is missed. Only #4 is affected: Burnley (H), 30 Aug 2025, 90+7'.
4. Every other goal and result stays the same; both 20-team tables are rebuilt (points, then goal difference, then goals scored).

## Check on the data
- Bruno's totals in the data match official figures: 2024/25 8 goals (3 pens) + 10 assists; 2025/26 9 goals (4 pens) + 21 assists (Premier League record).
- Rebuilt real tables match: United 15th on 42 pts (2024/25), 3rd on 71 pts (2025/26).

## Sensitivity (penalty rule)
- Stand-in scores all 7: 2024/25 17th, 33 pts; 2025/26 16th, 45 pts.
- Stand-in scores none: 2024/25 17th, 30 pts; 2025/26 17th, 39 pts (level on points with relegated West Ham, safe on goal difference).

## Limits
Deleting a goal doesn't replay the match: the opponent might have played differently, and someone else might have scored. The video states the rules on screen.

**Method:** Bruno's goals and assists are deleted as before. In each match the stand-in plays Bruno's real minutes and adds goals at his real rate (random, Poisson). He also takes all 9 penalties Bruno took (7 scored, 2 missed) at 75%. Every other result stays real. Both seasons are run 10,000 times and the 20-team table is rebuilt each time.
