# What if Bruno Fernandes had left Man United in summer 2024?

WHO IT'S FOR: United fans (and rivals who love a United argument). Angle: **"you're wrong" stakes claim** (United's third place was Bruno) plus a live debate (he's reportedly leaving).
Hook: "Without Bruno Fernandes, Man United don't make the Champions League." Frame 0: Bruno (8) close-up inside a SIMULATION frame; by ~1s he glitches and breaks into pixels;
then a finishing-position chart builds live and a "7%" lands on the Champions League places.
Re-hook at line 7 ("And that was the bad season"): VHS cut to 2025/26 and his 21-assist record. Ends on a split question: let him go?

## Method (full detail and data in research.md)
- Premier League 2024/25 and 2025/26, all 760 real results. Bruno's goals and assists are deleted (17 goals, 31 assists).
- His minutes go to the man who played that role: Mason Mount (both seasons) and Christian Eriksen (2024/25). They add goals at their own real
  non-penalty rate (0.16 per 90 in 2024/25, 0.27 in 2025/26) and take all 9 of Bruno's penalties at 75%. Every other result stays real.
- Each season is played 10,000 times and the full table is rebuilt every time.

## Numbers in the video
- 2024/25 real: 15th, 42 pts (United's lowest PL total; previous low 58 in 2021/22). Simulated: average 36 pts; 17th in 75% of runs, 16th in 21%; never relegated.
- 2025/26 real: 3rd, 71 pts. Simulated: average 53 pts; top five (the Champions League places that season, via England's European Performance Spot) in 7.0% of runs, top four 2.2%.
- Bruno 2025/26: 21 assists, a Premier League record. Rates per 90 (no penalties): Bruno 0.78 v Mount 0.27 = about a third.
- "Reports say he could leave this summer": Football365 / talkSPORT reports, not confirmed, so "could".

## Change from the approved draft
- The Premier League had **five** Champions League places in 2025/26 (European Performance Spot, confirmed April 2026). So the hook and line 10 use 7% (top five), not 2% (top four).
- Seasons are spoken as "twenty-four twenty-five" / "twenty-five twenty-six" (timeless and easy to voice).
