## Description
Without Bruno Fernandes, Man United don't make the Champions League 😳 We simulated it 10,000 times.

We took the last two Premier League seasons, deleted every goal Bruno scored or set up (17 goals, 31 assists), and gave his minutes to the men who actually played there: Mason Mount, plus Christian Eriksen in 2024/25. They scored at their own real rate and took his penalties. Then we played each season 10,000 times.
2024/25: real United finished 15th on 42 points, their worst Premier League season. With the stand-in: about 36 points, 17th in three runs out of four.
2025/26: real United finished 3rd on 71 points, with Bruno's record 21 assists. With the stand-in: about 53 points, and Champions League football (top five that season) in just 7 runs in 100.
