  Covers 2009/10 – 2017/18.   [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/13594069/man-city-charges-how-premier-league-club-plan-to-argue-their-innocence-in-appeal), [Yahoo](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/manchester-citys-830m-guilty-verdict-194748662.html)

  [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/13593606/man-city-lodge-appeal-premier-league-club-contest-guilty-verdict-of-independent-commission)
