## Copy-paste block

[serious, measured] Man City are top of the Premier League. They've also just been found guilty on one hundred and fourteen charges.
[authoritative] On the twenty-ninth of September, an independent commission finally ruled on a case dating back to February 2023.
[grave] The key finding: sham sponsorship deals inflated City's income by eight hundred and thirty million pounds, over nine seasons.
[matter-of-fact] Every single financial charge was proven. So were thirty-four of the thirty-five for failing to cooperate.
[neutral, fair] City deny any wrongdoing. On the first of October they appealed, claiming clear material errors of law, principle and fact.
[dry] And until that appeal is decided, there is no punishment at all.
[storytelling] For context, the biggest points deduction in Premier League history is ten. Everton, later cut to six.
[intrigued] Reports suggest City could be hit with forty to fifty.
[building tension] Opta's supercomputer ran it. Minus twenty, City stay up. Minus forty, relegation is more likely than not. Minus seventy-four, they go down every time.
[dramatic, low] Take forty off today, and the league leaders would be bottom, on minus nineteen.
[curious, inviting] So what should the punishment be? Points, titles, or relegation? Tell me in the comments.
