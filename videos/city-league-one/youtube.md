Part 2 of Man City in League Two: you picked him, City signed him. Tim Iroegbunam 🔵

Last episode, City (Haaland, Foden and the academy) won League Two. The comments voted for one signing: Tim Iroegbunam, the 23-year-old ball-winning midfielder (ex-Aston Villa and Everton, now at Hull, Under-19 European champion with England). He goes straight in next to Braybrooke. At halfway City are 12th: only one team has scored more, only two have let in more. Promotion chance: down to 38%.
A simulation, not a real season. Catch Part 1 on the channel.
