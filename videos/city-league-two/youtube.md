## Description
Man City have been relegated to League Two. Only Erling Haaland stayed… and the kids 😳

City keep Haaland, Phil Foden, Rico Lewis, Nico O'Reilly and the under-21s, plus four real League Two signings. Premier League under-21 sides already play League Two clubs in the EFL Trophy, and they've lost more than they've won (30 wins, 24 draws, 45 defeats). We simulated City's League Two season 10,000 times: they won the league in 9 out of 10. Then we played it once. City lost on the opening day at Gillingham, went top at Salford, dropped below Grimsby with four games left, lost 4–0 at home to Swindon and went into the final day level on 89 points. City beat Chesterfield 3–1, Grimsby lost 6–1 at Fleetwood, and City were champions by three points. Haaland: 29 goals.
This is a simulation, not a real season. Episode 2: League One.
