`videos/city-league-two/data/ep4.json` (seed 2029, fixed before running) and `ep4_extra.json`; McCracken's and the midfielders' numbers are real (ESPN, 23 Nov 2026;

(14 lines, 148 words; about 62 s at 1.08×).

## Three hooks (frame plans, virality checklist)
**A (picked): "Man City signed the keeper who beat them at Wembley."** Pattern: the villain joins you (personal conflict, and it is the viewers' own vote).
- 0.0s: Wembley, the play-off final from Part 3. Haaland's shot is already flying; every headline word is on screen; zoom burst and speed lines.
- 0.5s: the Bradford keeper (labelled McCracken) dives and holds it; "DENIED!" slams; the score bug reads BRA 2–0 MCI, FINAL.
- 1.2s: the claim lands ("the keeper who beat them"): a ring under him, a punch-in.
- 3.0s: cut to the poll: 43% and his shirt flips from Bradford to City.
- Checklist: moving on frame 0; a famous player in his real kit at a famous ground; the keeper and ball fill the middle of the frame; an extraordinary thing before 3s (a save and a stamp);
  the subject is the first spoken word ("Man City"); works on mute; it is the viewer's world (their vote).
**B: "Man City were ninety-four percent to go up. Then day one."** Pattern: something breaks (the number shatters). 94 of 100 squares already lit on frame 0; Stevenage's shot at 0.5s;
  the squares go red and a 0–1 stamp slams at 1.2s. Held back: the first image is not instantly recognisable and Stevenage is a weak villain. Day one became beat 6.
**C: "The best defence in League One belongs to Man City. They're fourth."** Pattern: contradiction. A counter racing to 22 with a 4TH stamp. Held back: it gives away the main result and it is a stat, not a person.
