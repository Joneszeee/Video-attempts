## Fact check (searched 2 Nov 2026)
- Kane won the 2026 men's Ballon d'Or, 26 Oct 2026, at the London Palladium. It was the first ceremony in London. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/50259091/2026-ballon-dor-live-updates-winners-results-reaction), [UEFA](https://www.uefa.com/ballondor/news/02a5-20bba5196317-e0e34001e13b-1000--2026-ballon-d-or-ceremony-date-and-host-city-announced/)
- Kane 1,055 points (40 first-place votes) v Mbappé 1,027 (23). "One of the smallest margins in history." [ESPN results](https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/50330001/2026-ballon-dor-results-men-full-women-votes-kane-mbappe)
- London was chosen as a tribute to Stanley Matthews, the first winner in 1956. [UEFA](https://www.uefa.com/ballondor/news/02a5-20bba5196317-e0e34001e13b-1000--2026-ballon-d-or-ceremony-date-and-host-city-announced/), [WTOP/AP](https://wtop.com/sports/2026/05/ballon-dor-awards-for-worlds-best-soccer-players-in-2026-moves-from-paris-to-london/)
- English winners: Matthews 1956, Charlton 1966, Keegan 1978 and 1979, Owen 2001. Kane is the fifth Englishman and the first in 25 years. [Wikipedia: 2026 Ballon d'Or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Ballon_d%27Or)
- Kane won no trophy at Tottenham. His first trophy was the 2024/25 Bundesliga with Bayern.
- 2025/26: 36 Bundesliga goals (top scorer). Hat-trick in the DFB-Pokal final, 3-0 v Stuttgart. Bundesliga and Pokal double. [Bundesliga.com](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/harry-kane-2025-26-numbers-england-bauern-munich-goals-records-messi-38301), [beIN](https://www.beinsports.com/en-nz/football/dfb-pokal/articles/kane-hat-trick-seals-domestic-double-for-bayern-2026-05-23)
- World Cup 2026: Kane scored 6 and became England's top World Cup scorer (passing Lineker). England finished third (6-4 v France), their best finish since 1966. [The Star/Reuters](https://www.thestar.com.my/sport/football/2026/06/28/soccer-kane-becomes-england039s-top-goalscorer-at-world-cups), [The Analyst](https://theanalyst.com/?p=137643)
- Not used because the sources disagree: his total goals for the season (61/73).
- Note: the FIFA Ballon d'Or galas of 2010–15 were held in Zurich, so we say "came to London for the first time", **not** "left Paris for the first time".

## Script
1. The Ballon d'Or came to London for the first time. And for the first time in twenty-five years, an Englishman won it.
2. Harry Kane beat Kylian Mbappé by just twenty-eight points. One of the closest votes ever.
3. Why London? Because the very first Ballon d'Or, in 1956, went to an Englishman: Stanley Matthews.
4. Since then, only three more Englishmen have won it. Bobby Charlton. Kevin Keegan, twice. And Michael Owen, in 2001.
5. Then, twenty-five years of nothing.
6. Kane spent his whole Tottenham career without winning a single trophy.
7. Then came Bayern. Thirty-six league goals, a hat-trick in the cup final, and a domestic double.
8. At the World Cup, six more goals. England finished third, their best since 1966.
9. Seventy years after Matthews won the first one, it came to London, and an Englishman won it again.
10. But did Kane really deserve it over Mbappé? Tell me in the comments.
