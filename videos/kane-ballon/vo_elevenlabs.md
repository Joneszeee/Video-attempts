[proud, building] The Ballon d'Or came to London for the first time. And for the first time in twenty-five years, an Englishman won it.
[impressed] Harry Kane beat Kylian Mbappé by just twenty-eight points. One of the closest votes ever.
[storytelling, warm] Why London? Because the very first Ballon d'Or, in 1956, went to an Englishman: Stanley Matthews.
[measured] Since then, only three more Englishmen have won it. Bobby Charlton. Kevin Keegan, twice. And Michael Owen, in 2001.
[flat, deadpan] Then, twenty-five years of nothing.
[sympathetic] Kane spent his whole Tottenham career without winning a single trophy.
[excited, rising] Then came Bayern. Thirty-six league goals, a hat-trick in the cup final, and a domestic double.
[proud] At the World Cup, six more goals. England finished third, their best since 1966.
[emotional, triumphant] Seventy years after Matthews won the first one, it came to London, and an Englishman won it again.
[curious, inviting] But did Kane really deserve it over Mbappé? Tell me in the comments.
