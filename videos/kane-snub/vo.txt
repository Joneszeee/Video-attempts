Monday the ninth of November, 2026. FIFPRO names its World Eleven, chosen by thousands of professional players.

One name is missing: Harry Kane.

<break time="0.8s" />
But here's the twist. That award is voted for by journalists. This team is picked by players.
<break time="0.8s" />
And among the forwards, Kane finished fifth.
<break time="0.8s" />
He got twenty-seven point six percent of the vote. Haaland got twenty-eight point four.
<break time="0.8s" />
He missed out by less than one percent.

No Ballon d'Or winner had ever been left out of the World Eleven. Until now.
