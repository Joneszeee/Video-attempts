## Fact check (verified 30 Oct 2026)
- Klose's 16th World Cup goal came in the 23rd minute of Brazil 1–7 Germany (8 July 2014, Mineirão, Belo Horizonte), taking him past Ronaldo's 15.
  [SI](https://www.si.com/soccer/2014/07/08/miroslav-klose-world-cup-goals-record-germany-brazil-ronaldo), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/amp/sports/2014/7/8/germany-crush-brazil-to-reach-final)
- Ronaldo was in the stadium working as a TV commentator. [Bleacher Report](https://bleacherreport.com/articles/2123408-miroslav-klose-breaks-ronaldo-record-to-compound-brazil-misery)
- Before the tournament, Ronaldo said he'd be cheering against Klose. [Washington Times / AP](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2014/jun/10/brazils-ronaldo-cheering-against-klose-at-wcup/)
- Klose equalled Ronaldo (15) against Ghana (2–2, 21 June 2014), scoring with his first touch off the bench. Ronaldo's own 15th came against Ghana in 2006.
  [MLS](https://mlssoccer.com/news/world-cup-germanys-miroslav-klose-scores-against-ghana-tie-ronaldos-all-time-goa)
- Klose's tally by tournament: 5 (2002) + 5 (2006) + 4 (2010) = 14 before 2014.
- Germany led 5–0 after 29 minutes, with four goals in six minutes; it finished 7–1. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil_v_Germany_(2014_FIFA_World_Cup))
- Klose didn't do his trademark somersault. [Bleacher Report](https://bleacherreport.com/articles/2123408-miroslav-klose-breaks-ronaldo-record-to-compound-brazil-misery)
- Germany won the final on 13 July 2014.
- 2026 World Cup: Mbappé finished on 22 all-time World Cup goals (a record) and Messi on 21. Spain beat Argentina 1–0 in the final.
  [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/7/18/kylian-mbappe-breaks-all-time-world-cup-scoring-record-with-22nd-goal), [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/49394546/fifa-world-cup-2026-stats-kylian-mbappe-tops-lionel-messi-22-michael-olise-breaks-pele-record-7-jude-bellingham-7-most-ever-england)
