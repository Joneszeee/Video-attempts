## Copy-paste block

[intrigued, low] Ronaldo watched his own World Cup record fall. In Brazil. During a seven-one.
[storytelling] In 2006, against Ghana, Ronaldo scored his fifteenth World Cup goal, and passed Gerd Müller.
[knowing, slight smile] Before Brazil 2014, he said he'd be cheering against one man. Miroslav Klose, on fourteen.
[building excitement] Then, against Ghana of all teams, Klose came off the bench and scored with his first touch. Fifteen.
[tense, anticipating] Seventeen days later, the semi-final. Brazil against Germany, in Belo Horizonte.
[excited, triumphant] Twenty-three minutes in, Klose scores. Sixteen. The record is his.
[quiet, poignant] And Ronaldo was in the stadium, working as a TV commentator.
[disbelieving] Then it got worse. Four German goals in six minutes. Five-nil after twenty-nine.
[sombre] It ended seven-one. Brazil's heaviest ever World Cup defeat.
[warm, admiring] And Klose? He didn't even do his famous somersault.
[proud, then reflective] Five days later, Germany were world champions. And Klose's sixteen stood for twelve years.
[building, then curious] Then, this summer, Messi and Mbappé blew past it. Mbappé now has twenty-two. Can anyone ever catch him?
