## Fact check
Confirmed (Manchester United's own site, Wikipedia, Spartacus Educational, Gardiner's Stafford biography via reviews):
- Newton Heath about £2,670 in debt; four-day bazaar at St James's Hall, 27 Feb – 2 Mar 1901, hoping to raise £1,000.
- Major, captain Harry Stafford's St Bernard, carried a collection box; he went missing and ended up with brewer John Henry Davies.
- Stafford told Davies about the club's debts; Davies kept the dog (his daughter's 12th birthday) and invested.
- January 1902 winding-up order; April 1902 renamed Manchester United; first league title 1908.
Left out (unsourced): the collar message, "lived to 17", the "club or your dog" quote, the Old Trafford banner. How Major reached Davies varies by
account, so the VO says "the story goes".
