## Description
Manchester United only exist because a dog got lost. 🐶

In 1901 the club was called Newton Heath and was about £2,670 in debt. Captain Harry Stafford's St Bernard, Major, carried a collection box round a fundraising bazaar that was meant to raise £1,000. It didn't. Then Major went missing and, the story goes, ended up with wealthy brewer John Henry Davies. Davies kept the dog for his daughter, heard about the club's debts from Stafford, and put his money in. In 1902 Newton Heath became Manchester United, and in 1908 they were champions of England.
