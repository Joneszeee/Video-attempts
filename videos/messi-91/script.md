## Fact check (verified 29 Oct 2026)
- Müller: 85 goals in calendar year 1972 (72 Bayern + 13 West Germany). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerd_M%C3%BCller)
- Messi's 86th goal: at Real Betis on 9 Dec 2012. [Guinness](https://guinnessworldrecords.com/news/2012/12/barcelona-star-lionel-messi-sets-new-goalscoring-record-46285)
- Messi's 2012 total: 91 (79 Barcelona + 12 Argentina), the official Guinness record. [Guinness](https://guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/106274-most-football-goals-scored-in-a-calendar-year)
- Zambia FA claimed Chitalu scored 107 in 1972 (49 league + 58 cup/internationals), compiled from Times of Zambia, Zambia Daily Mail and the national archives. [Irish Times](https://www.irishtimes.com/sport/zambia-claim-striker-godfrey-chitalu-scored-107-goals-in-1972-1.2751), [Arab News](https://arabnews.com/node/434225)
- FIFA said it couldn't verify the claim because it only keeps stats for FIFA competitions. [MyJoyOnline](https://www.myjoyonline.com/fifa-cant-verify-messi-record/)
- Messi scored in the 16th and 25th minutes at Betis; Iniesta's back-heel set up the second. [Sports Mole](https://www.sportsmole.co.uk/football/betis/match-analysis/real-betis-1-2-barcelona_58701.html)
- Flamengo claimed Zico scored 89 in 1979 (81 Flamengo, 7 Brazil, 1 friendly). [SI](https://www.si.com/soccer/2012/12/12/lionel-messi-record-zico)
- Chitalu was coaching Zambia when the squad died in the plane crash off Gabon on 27 April 1993. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Godfrey_Chitalu)
- Correction to the first draft: the original beat 5 said Zambia had no proof. That was wrong. They did have evidence; FIFA declined to verify it.
