Sixteen minutes in: eighty-five, level with Gerd Müller.
<break time="0.3s" />
Nine minutes later: eighty-six. The record is his.
<break time="0.3s" />
Müller's eighty-five had stood since 1972.
<break time="0.3s" />

Then Zambia spoke up. Their claim: Godfrey Chitalu scored one hundred and seven in 1972.

FIFA's answer? It couldn't verify it.
