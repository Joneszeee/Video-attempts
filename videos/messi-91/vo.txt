December 2012. Lionel Messi scores his eighty-sixth goal of the calendar year, and breaks a record that had stood for forty years.
<break time="0.8s" />
The old record belonged to Gerd Müller. Eighty-five goals in 1972.
<break time="0.8s" />
Messi finished the year on ninety-one. Seventy-nine for Barcelona, twelve for Argentina.
<break time="0.8s" />
Then Zambia's FA objected. They said Godfrey Chitalu scored one hundred and seven in 1972.
<break time="0.8s" />
Zambia had newspaper evidence. But FIFA said it couldn't verify the claim, and Guinness kept Messi's ninety-one.
<break time="0.8s" />
Müller, eighty-five. Messi, ninety-one. Chitalu, one hundred and seven. So who really holds the record?
