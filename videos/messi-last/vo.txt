Lionel Messi once bathed a baby for a charity photo. Nineteen years later, that baby ended his World Cup career.
<break time="0.8s" />
The photo was taken in 2007, for a UNICEF calendar. The baby was Lamine Yamal.
<break time="0.8s" />
July 2026. The World Cup final. Messi's Argentina against Yamal's Spain.
<break time="0.8s" />
Messi was thirty-nine, playing in his sixth World Cup.
<break time="0.8s" />
For ninety minutes, Argentina held on.
<break time="0.8s" />
Then, in the hundred-and-sixth minute, Ferran Torres scored. Spain won one–nil.
<break time="0.8s" />
Two days later, Messi wrote his goodbye letter.
<break time="0.8s" />
After two hundred and seven games and one hundred and twenty-five goals, he was done with Argentina.
<break time="0.8s" />
He'd quit once before, in 2016, and came back to win the World Cup and two Copa Américas.
<break time="0.8s" />
This time, the baby from the photo had the last word.
