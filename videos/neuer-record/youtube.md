## Description
On Saturday the 7th of November 2026, Manuel Neuer became Bayern's oldest ever Bundesliga player, at 40 years and 225 days. Forty minutes later, he was limping off. 🧤

Kimmich put Bayern ahead after 14 minutes, set up by Kane. Then Neuer came flying out for Amiri's free kick, missed it, and Lee Jae-sung scored behind him. Neuer hurt his right foot and Jonas Urbig came on, but VAR ruled Lee offside. Karl made it 2–0 and Tietz pulled one back late: Mainz 1–2 Bayern. Neuer passed Bernd Dreher's record of 40 years and 198 days from 2007.
