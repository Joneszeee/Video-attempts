# Nobody is catching Ronaldo's 146

WHO IT'S FOR: Ronaldo fans and everyone who argues about Kane, Mbappé and Haaland. Angle: **contrarian claim** ("will never be broken"), three big names, ends on a four-way poll.
Hook: "Cristiano Ronaldo's record will never be broken." Frame 0: Ronaldo (Portugal 7) celebrating, counter already racing to 146, locks at ~1.8s with a shake; UNBREAKABLE? stamp.
Re-hook at line 7 (VHS "BUT"): even Haaland's rate needs about eight years.

## Fact check (AP via Yahoo/The Week, 10 Nov 2026; UEFA's top-scorers page; CBS, Sky)
- Ronaldo retired from Portugal on 5 November 2026: 146 goals, 234 games, 23 years; first player to score at six World Cups. (One NBC affiliate says 255 games; most say 234.)
- Messi finished his Argentina career second all-time on 126; Ali Daei 108.
- Latest UEFA list: Kane 93, Mbappé 67, Haaland 66 (AP on 10 Nov had 91 / 67 / 65). Kane is 33 (born July 1993), Haaland 26 and "averages more than one goal per game" (AP).
- Gaps are arithmetic: 146−93 = 53, 146−67 = 79, 146−66 = 80.
- **"Around eight years" is our estimate**: 80 goals at about a goal a game, at roughly ten internationals a year. Shown on screen as "an estimate". AP's own view: the record is "likely safe until at least the 2030 World Cup".
- "Will never be broken" is the video's claim, not a fact; the ending asks the viewer.
