## Fact check
- 31 Oct 2026, Tottenham 2–0 Crystal Palace (home). Archie Gray header 25', Jan Paul van Hecke 73' (Henderson reflex save, off the underside of the bar
  and in off van Hecke). Mateta sent off early (13' in most reports, 10' in one) for a late studs-up tackle on Micky van de Ven; VAR upgraded yellow to red.
  Third straight league win under Roberto De Zerbi; Palace into the relegation zone. [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/tottenham-hotspur-vs-crystal-palace/report/559533),
  [Yahoo](https://uk.sports.yahoo.com/news/matetas-early-red-proves-costly-190837981.html), [Camden New Journal](https://www.camdennewjournal.co.uk/article/spurs-make-it-three-on-the-spin-with-win-over-10-man-palace)
- 759 completed passes, Spurs' most in a Premier League game (previous best 747 at Cardiff, Jan 2019). First reports said 758; the club's later version and
  most coverage say 759 (user chose 759). Pass accuracy 93.12%, a club record. Van Hecke 143 completed passes, a club record for a player.
  [Tottenham](https://www.tottenhamhotspur.com/news/1183604/759-premier-league-passing-record-broken-in-palace-win-premier-league-2026-27),
  [Spurs Web](https://www.spurs-web.com/spurs-news/two-record-breaking-passing-stats-prove-de-zerbis-tottenham-are-finally-purring/)
- Man City 902 completed passes v Chelsea, 4 March 2018 (1–0, Bernardo Silva 46'): the most by any Premier League team since Opta records began (2003/04).
  [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/11679/11276697/manchester-city-break-passing-records-in-1-0-win-over-chelsea),
  [Premier League](https://www.premierleague.com/news/636331)
- Not used: possession % and shot count (single sources), the "616 days" stat (single source), exact red-card minute (sources differ).

## Script
1. Tottenham just completed 759 passes in one game. Manchester City once made 902.
2. On Saturday, Palace came to Tottenham. Early on, Mateta was sent off for a late tackle on Van de Ven.
3. Against ten men, Spurs kept the ball. And kept it. And kept it.
4. Archie Gray headed in the first.
5. The second was saved, hit the bar, and went in off Jan Paul van Hecke.
6. By full time: 759 completed passes. The most in Spurs' Premier League history.
7. Van Hecke completed 143 of them on his own. Another club record.
8. And 93 per cent of their passes found a teammate. A club record too.
9. But rewind to 2018. Manchester City completed 902 passes against Chelsea.
10. It's still the most ever recorded in the Premier League.
