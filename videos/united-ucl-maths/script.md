# United's Champions League maths (toy / clay one-off style)

Built purely on the virality skill (.claude/skills/virality/SKILL.md).

WHO IT'S FOR: Manchester United fans. They get the exact maths for their club's Champions League run and a fixture list to argue about.
Angle: pain first, aimed at the viewer ("United fans: you need…"). Rotates from the last two (number clash, contrarian).

## Hooks (3 options, 0–3s frame plans, 8-point checklist)
**A. "United fans: you need ten points" ← PICKED** (pain first + names the viewer + curiosity: "look who's left")
  0.0s  Toy Fernandes (United red, 8) fills the frame, scowling, arm up, camera already pushing in. Old Trafford diorama in red.
  0.3s  First words: "United fans…"
  1.2s  A giant clay "10" drops from above and SLAMS onto the pitch (squash + shake). Headline lands word by word: "United fans: you need 10 points."
  3.0s  "…And look who's left." Four opponent toys drop onto the pitch behind him one by one, each with a name pill.
  CHECKLIST: 1 ✓ camera + toy moving on frame 0 · 2 ✓ United red, a United player · 3 ✓ toy ≥ 40% of frame · 4 ✓ clay 10 slams at 1.2s ·
             5 ✓ word by word · 6 ✓ "United fans" first · 7 ✓ "10 points" + four opponents read on mute · 8 ✓ the viewer's club, the viewer named
**B. "Most United fans think they're out. They're not."** (contrarian). Fails 8 slightly (tells the viewer what they think) and is unverifiable.
**C. "United's Champions League hinges on one number."** Curiosity, but no viewer, and no thing on screen until the number lands.

Rule 2: the viewer is named in line 1 ("United fans: you…") and again at the end ("comment your number").
Rule 3: every line has its own mechanism on screen (below). Rule 4: comment trigger = a number prediction (low effort, splits opinion).
Rule 5: angle rotated (pain first).
