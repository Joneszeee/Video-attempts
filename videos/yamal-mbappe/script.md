## Fact check (searched 2 Nov 2026)
All six knockout meetings were won by Yamal's team. [Fox Sports (first 5)](https://www.foxsports.com/stories/soccer/all-5-times-lamine-yamal-has-beaten-kylian-mbappe-elimination-matches), [Sofascore](https://www.sofascore.com/news/their-own-clasico-yamal-keeps-winning-the-big-knockout-duels-with-mbappe), [Bolavip](https://bolavip.com/en/world-cup/lamine-yamal-vs-kylian-mbappe-head-to-head-record-in-single-elimination-games-ahead-of-2026-world-cup-semifinal)
1. Euro 2024 semi-final, 9 Jul 2024: Spain 2–1 France. Yamal (16) scored a curling shot from outside the box.
2. Supercopa final, 12 Jan 2025: Barcelona 5–2 Real Madrid (Mbappé scored first).
3. Copa del Rey final, 26 Apr 2025: Barcelona 3–2 Real Madrid after extra time (Mbappé scored a free kick).
4. Nations League semi-final, 5 Jun 2025: Spain 5–4 France. Yamal scored twice (Mbappé scored a penalty).
5. Supercopa final, 11 Jan 2026: Barcelona 3–2 Real Madrid. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Supercopa_de_Espa%C3%B1a_final)
6. World Cup semi-final, 14 Jul 2026: Spain 2–0 France. [Reuters/The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/sport/football/2026/07/15/soccer-spain-beat-france-2-0-to-reach-world-cup-final)
- Ballon d'Or 2026: Mbappé second, 28 points behind Kane (1,027 v 1,055). [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/50330001/2026-ballon-dor-results-men-full-women-votes-kane-mbappe)
