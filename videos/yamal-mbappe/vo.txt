Kylian Mbappé has faced Lamine Yamal six times in knockout football. He's lost all six.
<break time="0.8s" />
It started at Euro 2024. A sixteen-year-old Yamal curled one in, and Spain knocked France out in the semi-final.
<break time="0.8s" />
January 2025, the Spanish Super Cup final. Barcelona five, Real Madrid two.
<break time="0.8s" />
April, the Copa del Rey final. Barcelona win three–two in extra time.
<break time="0.8s" />
June, the Nations League semi-final. Spain five, France four. Yamal scores twice.
<break time="0.8s" />
January 2026, another Super Cup final. Barcelona win again, three–two.
<break time="0.8s" />
Then the biggest one of all. A World Cup semi-final. Spain two, France nil.
<break time="0.8s" />
Six knockout games. Six defeats.
<break time="0.8s" />
The strangest part? Mbappé even scored in some of them. It never mattered.
